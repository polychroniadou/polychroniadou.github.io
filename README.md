# Hi, I'm Maria 👋

MSc student in **Geography & Applied Geoinformatics** at the University of the Aegean, specializing in remote sensing, deep learning, and heritage conservation. My thesis focuses on automated damage detection on cultural heritage monuments using Convolutional Neural Networks (CNNs) and UAV datasets. I hold a BA in Heritage Management with practical experience in archaeological fieldwork.

## Technical Toolkit

- **GIS & Spatial Analysis:** ArcGIS Pro, QGIS, PostGIS / Spatial SQL
- **Remote Sensing & EO:** Multispectral processing, Sentinel-2 (Copernicus), DEM/DSM extraction
- **UAV Photogrammetry & LiDAR:** DJI Mavic 3 Enterprise, Zenmuse L1 LiDAR, Agisoft Metashape, CloudCompare
- **Programming & Data Science:** Python (`arcpy`, `geopandas`, `rasterio`), MATLAB, R, SPSS

## Featured Projects

- **[Multi-Scale UAV Mapping — Lesvos Geopark](https://github.com/polychroniadou/Lesvos-Geopark)**  
  High-resolution UAV photogrammetry (Mavic 3E) and aerial LiDAR (Matrice / Zenmuse L1) survey of UNESCO geoheritage fossil reserves, featuring 3D mesh modeling, DEM contouring, and Cloud-to-Cloud (C2C) alignment in CloudCompare and ArcGIS Pro.

- **[Cairo Urban Expansion (2015–2025)](https://github.com/polychroniadou/Cairo-Egypt-Urban-Expansion-2015---2025)**  
  Decadal built-environment change detection across Greater Cairo from Sentinel-2 multi-spectral imagery, utilizing a vectorized Artificial Neural Network (ANN) and GIS spatial analysis to quantify 532 km² of urban sprawl.

- **[Wildfire Ignition Risk Susceptibility](https://github.com/polychroniadou/Fire-Risk-Map)**  
  Spatial logistic regression model assessing wildfire ignition probability across Lesvos, Greece, from topographical and human-infrastructure variables in ArcGIS Pro and Python.
